About

Chemweno Top Achievers School is a leading educational institution dedicated to providing quality education and fostering academic excellence. Our mission is to empower students with knowledge, skills, and values that will enable them to succeed in their future endeavors.

Academics

At Chemweno Top Achievers School, we offer a comprehensive curriculum that caters to the diverse needs of our students. Our dedicated faculty members are committed to nurturing a love for learning and helping students reach their full potential.

Lower Primary

Building strong foundation in literacy,numeracy,creativity and social development.

Upper Primary

Developing academic skills,independence and preparation for the next stage of education.

Curriculum Activities

Encouraging learners to discover their talents through sports,arts and other activities

Admissions
